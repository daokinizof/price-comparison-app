## ENGLISH

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the Price Comparison Application ("the App", "we") collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and securing your data.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### 2.1 Information Stored Locally Only
- **Search History**: Searches you perform in the App are saved **only on your device** using browser Local Storage.
- **User Preferences**: Language choices, display mode (dark/light), and other settings are stored locally only.
- **No Server Storage**: We do **not** save your search history or preferences on our servers.

#### 2.2 Information We Do Not Collect
- We do **not** collect personally identifiable information (name, address, phone number, email).
- We do **not** track your browsing habits outside the App.
- We do **not** use cookies for tracking.

#### 2.3 Anonymous Technical Information
- **Streamlit Analytics**: The hosting platform (Streamlit Cloud) may collect basic technical data such as:
  - IP address (for security purposes only)
  - Browser and device type
  - App access time
  - General country/region

### 3. Use of Information

#### 3.1 Purposes of Use
- **Improving User Experience**: Search history (stored locally) allows quick access to previous searches.
- **Technical Maintenance**: Anonymous technical information is used to improve App performance and fix issues.
- **No Commercial Use**: We do **not** sell or share personal information with third parties.

### 4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

#### 4.1 Affiliate Links
- When you click a link to an external store, **you leave our App**.
- The external store (Amazon, AliExpress, etc.) may collect information according to its privacy policy.
- We do **not** control or take responsibility for external website privacy policies.

#### 4.2 Streamlit Cloud
- The App is hosted on Streamlit Cloud.
- Streamlit may collect basic technical information as detailed in section 2.3.
- For more information: [Streamlit Privacy Policy](https://streamlit.io/privacy-policy)

#### 4.3 No Active Sharing
- We do **not** share, sell, or rent your information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

### 5. Information Security

#### 5.1 Security Measures
- **HTTPS**: All communication with the App is encrypted using HTTPS.
- **Local Storage**: Search history is stored only on your device and not transmitted to servers.
- **No User Database**: We have no database containing user personal information.

#### 5.2 User Responsibility
- You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and browser.
- If using a public computer, consider clearing browser history after use.

### 6. User Rights

#### 6.1 Data Deletion
- **Local Deletion**: You can delete search history at any time through the App or by clearing browser Local Storage.
- **No Recovery**: Since we don't store information on servers, we cannot recover deleted history.

#### 6.2 Access to Information
- Since all information is stored locally on your device, you have full access to it at all times.

#### 6.3 Data Portability
- You can export your search history through the App's export function (if available).

### 7. Children's Privacy
- The App is not intended for children under 13.
- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
- If a parent or guardian discovers their child has provided information, please contact us.

### 8. External Links
- The App contains links to external websites (stores, products).
- We are **not responsible** for the privacy policies of these sites.
- We recommend reading the privacy policy of every website you visit.

### 9. Changes to Privacy Policy
- We reserve the right to update this policy at any time.
- Material changes will be posted in the App.
- The last update date appears at the top of this document.

### 10. Contact
For questions or requests regarding the Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App.

### 11. Regulatory Compliance

#### 11.1 GDPR (European Data Protection Regulation)
- Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, the App meets basic GDPR requirements.
- No explicit consent is needed as there is no personal data processing.

#### 11.2 Israeli Privacy Protection Law
- The App complies with Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981.
- No collection, storage, or processing of personal information without consent.
